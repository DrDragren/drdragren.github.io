About Me

Francis Aaron Milano

@DrDragren

Creating something and successfully doing so have always fascinated me. This was what started me in my path as a Programmer and Designer. Although my artistic skills are close to non existent, I am proud to say that I can still create and generate fresh ideas that can attract people's attention.
